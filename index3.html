📖 UNO Rules

Objective

Be the first player to get rid of all your cards. The first player with 0 cards wins!

How to Play

  • Each player starts with 7 cards.
  • On your turn, play a card that matches the top card's color or value.
  • If you can't play, draw from the deck. If the drawn card is playable, it plays automatically.
  • If the drawn card still can't be played, your turn passes.

Special Cards

  • 🚫 Skip — In 2-player, your turn continues (opponent is skipped).
  • 🔄 Reverse — In 2-player, acts like a Skip; your turn continues.
  • +2 — Opponent draws 2 cards and loses their turn.
  • WILD — Change the active color to any color you choose.
  • +4 — Change color AND opponent draws 4 cards. Can only be played legally if you have NO cards matching the current color.

Wild +4 Challenge

When your opponent plays Wild +4, you can challenge them. If they had a card matching the current color (meaning the +4 was illegal), they draw 4 instead of you. If the play was legal, you draw 6.

UNO Button

  • When you are down to 1 card, tap UNO! before playing it.
  • If you forget to say UNO and your opponent catches you, you draw 2 penalty cards.
  • Tap UNO! when your opponent has 1 card to catch them — they draw 2!

Winning

Play your last card and you win! Make sure you said UNO! first, or you'll receive a 2-card penalty and the game continues.

How to Start a Game

  • Host: Copy your invite link and share it. Once opponent connects, press ⚡ Start Game.
  • Guest: Paste the link or Room ID and click Connect & Play.
